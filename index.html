Translate a Witcher 3 mod menu

Thank you for helping. Nothing is uploaded: everything you type stays in your browser until you download the file.

How this works (30 seconds)
  • Each box is one line of the mod menu. The English is shown above it. Type your translation underneath.
  • Leave a box empty to keep the English for that line. You do not have to do them all.
  • Keep anything in angle brackets exactly as it is, for example <font size="16">. Keep characters like % and | too.
  • Menu space is limited. If a box shows an orange warning, your text is much longer than the English and may be cut off in game; shorten it if you can.
  • Your work is saved in this browser automatically. When finished, press Download CSV (or Copy CSV text) and send it to us (see the bottom of the page).
Sending it back

Please include the mod name and your language. If you want to be credited, say how your name should be written.
